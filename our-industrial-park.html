PRESTON INDUSTRIAL AND LOGISTICS PARK, BECHARAJI





With an unparalleled construction acumen, we has firmly cemented the position as an industry vanguard in developing world-class infrastructural industrial parks that attract multinational corporations (MNCs) across prime locations globally. The crowning achievement is a state-of-the-art Preston Industrial and Logistics park, Becharaji strategically situated right in front of the renowned Suzuki manufacturing plant, offering unmatched prime location advantages and seamless access for automotive industries in the Becharaji, Vitthalpur and Dekavada regions.

  • WORLD CLASS FACILITY

    we construct world-class industrial warehouses optimized for space, efficiency, and security. Our state-of-the-art facilities feature cutting-edge storage solutions and material handling systems, tailored to elevate your operational capabilities.

  • BUILT-TO-SUIT

    Our "Built to Suit" approach ensures that your industrial warehouse is meticulously designed and constructed to align with your specific requirements. From customized layouts and specialized storage systems to integrated material handling equipment, we work closely with you to create a facility that seamlessly supports your workflow and maximizes productivity.

  • TURN-KEY PROJECT

    With our turnkey approach, you can focus on your core operations while we handle all the intricate details, including site selection, design, permitting, construction, and final delivery. Our streamlined process minimizes delays and ensures that your world-class facility is delivered on time and within budget
