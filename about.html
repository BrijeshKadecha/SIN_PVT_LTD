About

OUR CHAIRMAN OUR COMPANY

Our Chairman:

Pioneer in developing spaces since 25+ years, Mr. Vijay had a humble beginnings from southern Gujarat and moved to Ahmedabad 20 years ago to reach better heights and gain new industrial prospects.

He is a visionary person with utmost simplicity and aim to do better everyday and always. He is amongst the few persons in Gujarat who have constructed 15+ Million sq. ft spaces in a small span of time.

Along with managing Shyam Infranetwork, he is a Karyakarta of Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) and is the active board member of the education wing of Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh called Vidhya Bharati which has one of the largest private network of 20,000+ schools in Bharat.

He is also the Vice President of All India Soni Samaj Mahamandal of Hindu Vaishnav caste.


हमारे अध्यक्ष:

विजय ढोलकिया

25+ वर्षों से विकासशील स्थानों में अग्रणी, श्री विजय ने दक्षिणी गुजरात से शुरुआत की थी और बेहतर ऊंचाइयों तक पहुंचने और नई औद्योगिक संभावनाएं हासिल करने के लिए 20 साल पहले अहमदाबाद पहुंचे।

वह अत्यंत सरल स्वभाव वाले एक दूरदर्शी व्यक्ति हैं और उनका लक्ष्य हर दिन और हमेशा बेहतर करने का है। वह गुजरात के उन कुछ लोगों में से हैं जिन्होंने बहुत कम समय में 15 मिलियन वर्ग फुट से अधिक जगह का निर्माण किया है।

श्याम इन्फ्रानेटवर्क के प्रबंधन के साथ-साथ, वह राष्ट्रीय सवयंसेवक संघ (आरएसएस) के कार्यकर्ता हैं और राष्ट्रीय सवयंसेवक संघ की शिक्षा शाखा विद्या भारती के सक्रिय बोर्ड सदस्य हैं, जिसका भारत में 20,000+ स्कूलों का सबसे बड़ा निजी नेटवर्क है।

वह हिंदू वैष्णव जाति के अखिल भारतीय सोनी समाज महामंडल के उपाध्यक्ष भी हैं।


Our Company:


Shyam Infranetwork has been a pioneering force in the civil construction industry, taking immense pride in our rich history spanning over two decades of delivering exceptional construction solutions.

Established in the year 2001, our company has emerged as a leader, renowned for our unwavering commitment to excellence, innovation, and customer satisfaction. With a strong emphasis on sustainability and environmental responsibility, we strive to create built environments that harmonize with nature and contribute to a greener future.

Our forte lies in industrial construction, where we have earned a reputation for executing complex projects with precision and efficiency. In fact, our capabilities extend far beyond, as we have successfully undertaken various initiatives, including the development of schools, temples, and corporate social responsibility (CSR) projects for multinational companies.

At the core of our success lies our adherence to the highest standards of quality, safety, and sustainability. We meticulously adhere to all relevant regulations, ensuring that every project meets or exceeds industry norms.

Our team of highly skilled professionals collaborates seamlessly, fostering an environment of open communication, transparency, and accountability. Together, we strive to deliver projects on time, within budget, and with unwavering attention to detail.

We believe that true success is achieved through a collaborative approach, where we work in tandem with our clients, partners, and stakeholders. By actively listening to their needs and concerns, we tailor our solutions to meet their unique requirements, ensuring lasting value and customer satisfaction.
